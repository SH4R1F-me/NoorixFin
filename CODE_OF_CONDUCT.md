# Code of Conduct

NoorixFin contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, harassment-free
community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual
identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

Expected conduct includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for privacy,
graceful acceptance of correction, and focus on what benefits the community.
Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, intimidation, sexualized attention,
personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or publishing another
person's private information without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
exclude participants whose conduct is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical; conflicts of
interest will be recused. Enforcement may range from a private correction to a
permanent ban based on impact and recurrence.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
